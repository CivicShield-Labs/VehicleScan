# VehicleScan

Part of **CivicShield Labs**.

AI-assisted vehicle inspection imaging and anomaly-analysis research platform.

## Status

Early-stage private research and development project.

## Principles

- Human authorization for consequential law-enforcement actions
- Safety-first system behavior
- Privacy by design
- Least-privilege access
- Strong authentication and authorization
- Tamper-evident auditability
- Explicit legal and policy boundaries
- Simulation before real-world deployment
- No unauthorized vehicle, device, network, or sensor access
- No circumvention of warrants, due process, or other legal requirements
